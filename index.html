Meet the Original Stormwalker

About Kelly

Kelly Trett-Thomason, CHt., is an author, Spiritual Empowerment Coach, Reiki Practitioner, and creator of The Stormwalker Method™.

Kelly works with people moving through grief, transition, burnout, spiritual awakening, and the kind of stuckness that does not lift just because someone says, “think positive.” Her work is grounded, intuitive, and practical.

Sessions may weave guided statework, energy awareness, simple somatic tools, spiritual perspective, and next steps that can actually be used day to day.

Rooted in Appalachian heritage, Kelly is also committed to preserving the mountain ways—the folk medicine, household wisdom, spiritual practices, stories, and practical knowledge carried through families and communities. That living tradition informs her work today and the books she is preparing for future generations.

The goal is not dependence or fixing. Kelly walks beside people while they recognize patterns, return to center, and strengthen trust in their own ability to navigate.
